VLA-Sync / method landscape

Research focus

Reliability is part of the model story.

A VLA policy can produce plausible actions while a runtime still executes the wrong time slice, accepts a malformed response, or hides the cost of a missed deadline. My work makes those boundaries visible.

  • 01Temporal alignmentMeasure observation age and choose the action suffix that is still live.
  • 02Guarded executionValidate, hold, brake, and fail closed when a policy response is not safe to publish.
  • 03Evidence disciplineKeep simulation pilots, offline replays, and hardware claims separate and inspectable.
